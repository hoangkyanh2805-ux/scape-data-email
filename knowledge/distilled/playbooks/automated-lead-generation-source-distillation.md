## One-Line Thesis

Lead generation can be automated by finding people already likely to buy, collecting recent evidence about their demand, creating buyer/property-specific assets, and stopping for human approval before spend or outreach.

## Mechanism

1. Identify who already buys or visibly needs a trade.
2. Pull recent source evidence from communities, public records, imagery, listings, or form responses.
3. Convert that evidence into buyer language, ad angles, property renders, or lead samples.
4. Place the asset where the buyer or business owner will see it.
5. Capture responses and report results.

## Reusable Principles

- Evidence beats persona guessing.
- Three months of audience language is current enough to reveal repeated complaints without becoming stale.
- One qualifier keeps local candidate scanning from compounding guesses.
- A property-specific render is the conversion asset for local trades.
- A sample of real nearby leads is stronger than a generic pitch to service businesses.
- Human approval gates protect spend, sending, publishing, claims, and lead transfer.

## Frameworks

Digital product: product -> buyer profile -> buyer communities -> three-month scrape -> profile counts -> angles/hooks -> creatives -> ad draft -> daily report.

Physical local: trade + city -> visible signal -> public source -> one qualifier -> candidate table -> render -> property page -> outreach batch -> response log.

Lead resale: captured leads -> local businesses -> coverage match -> three-lead sample -> outreach draft -> price -> dashboard delivery.

## Constraints

- Public data may be stale.
- Some imagery sources expose date metadata and others do not.
- Ads can be rejected for unsupported claims.
- Business owners may need follow-ups.
- Outreach and paid operations require approval.
- Contact data, privacy, consent, and local marketing laws must be respected.
