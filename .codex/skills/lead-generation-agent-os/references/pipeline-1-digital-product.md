# Pipeline 1: Digital Product Leads

Use when the user has a digital product, paid community, course, SaaS, template, serviceized offer, or checkout page.

## Goal

Create ads and a reporting loop from buyer language instead of generic positioning.

Flow: product -> buyer hypothesis -> three-month voice-of-customer scrape -> profile -> creative plan -> ad draft -> daily report.

## Steps

1. Product understanding: extract price, promise, mechanism, target outcome, objections, checkout URL, and claims. Separate demographics from psychographics.
2. Buyer habitat map: list specific subreddits, Facebook groups, YouTube channels, TikTok accounts, Discords, forums, newsletters, or communities. Mark unverified or inactive places as review.
3. Three-month data pull: collect posts, comments, replies, questions, complaints, paid alternatives, and outcome language. Preserve source URL, date, engagement, and platform.
4. Buyer profile: sort repeated complaints, desired outcomes, failed attempts, paid alternatives, objections, and exact phrases by count.
5. Creative plan: use the three highest-count complaints or wants as angles. Write three hooks per angle using audience language. Specify video concept, still concept, format, placement, and destination URL.
6. Ad draft and reporting: prepare one campaign with one ad set per angle and three creatives per angle unless the platform requires otherwise. Track spend, clicks, sales, cost per sale, revenue, and winning angle.

## Checks

- Every angle maps to source evidence.
- No fake testimonials, invented income claims, guaranteed outcomes, or unsupported regulated claims.
- Checkout URL and offer are consistent across creatives.
- Budget, conversion event, attribution source, and reporting cadence are explicit.

## Outputs

- buyer-profile
- community-source-map
- voice-of-customer-counts
- creative-plan
- ad-campaign-draft
- daily-performance-report

## Human Approval Gates

- Scraping private or restricted communities.
- Generating paid media assets at scale.
- Launching ads or changing budget.
- Publishing claims.
- Contacting visitors or abandoned checkouts.
